What we've seen

Four ways Indians get shortchanged, over and over.

◈

Spurious products

Fakes and counterfeits sold with genuine packaging, genuine invoices, and zero accountability when they fail.

◔

Deficient service

Money paid upfront, promises made in writing, and a service that never quite delivers what it committed to.

▣

Defective goods

Products that break, malfunction, or arrive damaged — and a seller who suddenly stops responding.

⚖

Unfair trade practices

Bait pricing, false claims, hidden terms — tactics designed to win a sale, not earn trust.

The real problem

It's not that Indians don't have rights. It's that almost no one can act on them in time.

Even where the law is clear, the system enforcing it is buried. And where the system is slow, most people never file at all — the process feels built for advocates, not for someone holding a receipt and a grievance. Companies learn that complaints simply go away.

What we believe

This shouldn't be this hard.

Every Indian has the right to be heard — not just the ones who can afford to wait it out.

A broken product or a broken promise deserves a real remedy, not a shrug and a support ticket.

You shouldn't need a law degree to use a law that was written for you.

Why it matters

Every claim filed does two things.

It gets you what you're owed.

A refund, a repair, a replacement, or compensation — the resolution the law already entitles you to, without needing to fight the process alone.

It makes the next person's fight shorter.

One complaint is a data point. A thousand identical complaints against the same company is a pattern that regulators, commissions, and courts can't ignore. Every case filed adds pressure the system has to respond to.

We're not just here to win your case. We're here to make winning less necessary.

Where this goes

The real power isn't one claim. It's every identical claim, pointed at the same company at once.

A brand can absorb a single complaint — one refund, quietly, is cheaper than fixing the problem. What it cannot absorb is two hundred of the same complaint arriving together, with a combined liability attached and a coordinated notice behind it. That is the shift Consumer X is built for. We don't just help you file; we find everyone the same company wronged the same way, group your claims, and turn scattered grievances into concentrated leverage the company has to answer.

You file alone.

The company weighs one small claim against years of court delay, and waits you out.

You file together.

One shared file, one number, one notice on behalf of everyone. Now the delay is the company's problem, not yours.

The pattern gets fixed.

Repeated, visible, coordinated claims make mistreating customers cost more than treating them right.
