Madam/Sir,

1. My client purchased a Vayu 8kg front-load washing machine (Invoice VA-2201) for a consideration of ₹24,999. Within three weeks of purchase, the machine developed a drum/motor defect and has since failed to complete any wash cycle.

2. Your authorised service centre inspected the machine on two occasions and has acknowledged the motor defect in writing. Despite this, and despite repeated follow-ups over four months, you have refused replacement or refund.

3. The above constitutes a defect in goods within the meaning of Section 2(10), actionable under Section 2(6)(a) of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

4. My client hereby calls upon you, within 30 days of receipt of this notice, to refund the consideration of ₹24,999 together with ₹1,800 incurred in consequential losses, failing which she will institute a complaint before the District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission, Pune — where, you may note, claims of this value attract no court fee — seeking refund, compensation for deficiency and harassment, and costs.

5. This notice is issued without prejudice to all other rights and remedies available to my client, which are expressly reserved.
