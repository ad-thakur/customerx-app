The process

Three stages, one continuous case file.

File and find out

Enter the details of your case and upload evidence. We tell you, free, which Commission your claim belongs in, how long you have left to file, and exactly what to do next.

02

No response? Pay a single upfront fee and we assemble the filing, involve an advocate, and carry it through to resolution.

Seven statutory grounds

If your case fits one of these, you likely have a claim.

Every complaint accepted under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 falls under Section 2(6). We ask a few questions and match your situation automatically.

Sec. 2(6)(a)

Defective goods

The product you bought is faulty, broken, or does not work as it should.

Sec. 2(6)(b)

Deficient service

A service you paid for was incomplete, delayed, or below the standard promised.

Sec. 2(6)(c)

Unfair trade practice

The seller misrepresented facts, used deceptive tactics, or misled you.

Sec. 2(6)(d)

Overcharging / excess pricing

You were charged more than the price displayed, agreed, or legally permitted.

Sec. 2(6)(e)

Spurious goods sold as genuine

What you received was fake, counterfeit, or not what was represented.

Sec. 2(6)(f)

Hazardous goods sold knowingly

The goods were unsafe or non-compliant with safety standards, and the seller knew.

Sec. 2(6)(g)

Misleading advertisement

An advertisement made false or misleading claims that influenced your purchase.
